## 1. Company Overview

Ammar AI Automation Solution is an AI automation and systems integration studio. We design, build, and maintain intelligent workflows that connect business software, customer communication channels, and AI agents into systems that run reliably without constant manual effort.

Our work sits at the intersection of workflow automation, applied artificial intelligence, and customer relationship management. We help small and mid-sized businesses replace repetitive operational tasks with automated processes that capture leads, respond to customers, organize data, and report on results.

**Mission:** To give growing businesses the operational capacity of a much larger team through practical, well-engineered automation.

**Vision:** To become a trusted automation partner for service businesses across South Asia and the wider Middle East.

**Core Values:**

- **Reliability over novelty.** We build systems that work on a Tuesday afternoon as well as they do in a demo.
- **Deterministic logic where it matters.** Business rules, date calculations, and pricing decisions are handled by code, not by language model guesswork.
- **Transparency.** Clients understand how their systems work and are never locked into opaque processes.
- **Continuous learning.** Our team builds and tests in real projects so that our methods keep pace with fast-moving tools.

## 2. Services

### 2.1 AI Agent Development

We design and deploy AI agents that handle defined tasks such as customer support, order status queries, appointment booking, lead qualification, and content research. Each agent is scoped with clear tools, guardrails, and escalation paths to a human team member.

### 2.2 Workflow Automation (n8n)

We build and host workflows on n8n, both on n8n Cloud and on self-hosted infrastructure. Services include workflow architecture, node configuration, custom Code node logic, error handling, and ongoing monitoring.

### 2.3 CRM Systems and GoHighLevel Implementation

We configure GoHighLevel accounts for service businesses, including sub-accounts, pipelines, opportunity stages, automated lead nurturing sequences, funnels, calendars, and white-label branded domains.

### 2.4 Messaging and Voice Channels

We integrate the WhatsApp Business API, including webhook configuration, permanent system user tokens, and AI-driven handling of text, voice notes, and images. We also build voice agents using ElevenLabs, connected to backend workflows through secure webhooks.

### 2.5 Social Media and Content Automation

We build multi-platform publishing pipelines for LinkedIn, Facebook, and Instagram, with AI-assisted research, drafting, image generation, and human-in-the-loop approval through Telegram or spreadsheet-based review.

### 2.6 Email Automation and Support Routing

We design email handling systems that classify incoming messages, retrieve answers from knowledge bases, process orders or returns, and send structured replies, with escalation for complex cases.

### 2.7 Knowledge Systems and Retrieval

We build retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems using vector databases such as Pinecone and Supabase so that AI agents answer from a client's own documents and data.

### 2.8 Infrastructure and Hosting Support

We set up and maintain self-hosted automation environments on cloud virtual private servers, including Docker configuration, reverse proxy and SSL management, domain configuration, and resource monitoring.

### 2.9 AI Development Training and Consulting

We advise teams on adopting AI coding tools and automation platforms, including Claude Code setup, reusable skills, project configuration files, and workflow governance.

## 4. Skills and Expertise

**Automation Architecture:** Designing multi-branch workflows with clear routing, error handling, and logging.

**Agent Engineering:** Structuring AI agents with defined tools, system prompts, structured JSON outputs, and human approval checkpoints.

**Integration Engineering:** Connecting third-party APIs, managing authentication, and troubleshooting webhook, token, and permission issues.

**CRM Configuration:** Building pipelines, automations, forms, calendars, and funnels in GoHighLevel, including payment gateway setup and custom code for interface adjustments.

**Retrieval Systems:** Chunking documents, generating embeddings, storing vectors, and tuning retrieval for accurate agent responses.

**Systems Administration:** Deploying and securing self-hosted services, configuring reverse proxies and certificates, and managing storage and uptime.

**Client Communication:** Translating technical systems into plain-language documentation, handover notes, and operational guides.

## 5. Engagement Process

1. **Discovery.** We document the client's business processes, tools, data sources, and goals.
2. **Architecture.** We design the system, identify deterministic and AI-driven components, and agree on the scope.
3. **Build.** We develop workflows and agents in iterative stages with regular review points.
4. **Testing.** We test edge cases, failure paths, and integrations before any live deployment.
5. **Launch and Handover.** We deploy, document the system, and train the client's team.
6. **Ongoing Support.** We monitor performance, update integrations, and extend workflows as the business grows.
